## 1. What it is

A portfolio — but the word "portfolio" undersells it. The goal is **a world you
enter**, not a page you scroll. Every section should feel like a place with its
own weather.

It has to work as well on a phone as on a desktop. Not "responsive as an
afterthought" — the mobile version gets its own interaction language.

## 3. The feel

- Heavy on **interactive experiences**, interesting visuals, and transitions.
  Being on the site should itself be an aesthetic experience.
- **Each page is its own experience — closer to a game than a page.**
- **Pages can look nothing like each other.** Candidate per-section treatments:
  shot-on-camera / filmic, video game, pixel art, and others TBD. The through
  line is authorship, not a shared stylesheet.
- **Wording is part of the design.** Intentional, clever synonyms for the
  ordinary labels — no "Home / About / Work / Contact."
- **Generative AI is fair game as a production tool** for site visuals, textures,
  backgrounds, transitions, character/world art.
